What we learned

1 · Filler misleads scGPTReal names, random expression: this misleads a model that reads gene names. Without it, whole-human scGPT matches PCA.
2 · Patient signal was diffusePCA grouped patients by summing small offsets over 1,500 genes. Biology or nuisance? Real data will tell.
3 · Some metrics lieWith 4 patients per drug, leaving one out tilts k-NN against its class. Drug needs a patient-level test.
